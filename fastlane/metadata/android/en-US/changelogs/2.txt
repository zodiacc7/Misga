Beta of Ramesh 1.1.

- Tabs have a single Telegram-style editor: name, senders and "show only" filters (spam / not spam / contacts / unknown) in one screen; the filter sheet no longer closes itself.
- Swipe actions on messages: choose Off / Mark as read / Delete for each direction.
- Delivery reports are off by default.
- The whole interface is now translated (English and Persian, following the system language).
- Beta builds are published as GitHub prereleases.
